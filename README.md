# Krishi-Price 🌾

A simple, highly visual mobile application built to empower rural farmers—especially those without a formal education—by providing instant access to accurate, updated, and region-specific crop market prices (Mandi rates).

## 🛑 The Problem
Many small-scale farmers in rural regions work hard to grow quality crops but lack direct access to daily market rates. Government portals are often text-heavy and too complex to navigate. As a result, farmers frequently rely on middle-men and are forced to sell their harvest far below the actual market value.

## 💡 Our Solution
**Krishi-Price** bridges this gap using a clean, icon-based no-code design that requires minimal reading:
- **Region-Specific Pricing:** Farmers can easily tap on their state or region.
- **Visual Interface:** Simple, clean crop pictures (Rice, Wheat, Potato) designed for easy navigation.
- **Accurate Rates:** Displays updated market prices in bold, clear numbers to ensure absolute clarity.

## 🛠️ Built With

- **Glide:** For creating the frontend user interface.
- **Google Sheets:** Used as a lightweight, cloud-based database to store crop pricing rows.
